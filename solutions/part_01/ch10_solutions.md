1. \(40(6)=240\) litres.
2. Cases, because cases per hour multiplied by hours gives cases.
3. \(2(1)+4(1)=6\).
4. \(4(1)+6(1)=10\).
5. \((2+4)/2+(4+6)/2=8\).
6. Values below the horizontal axis contribute negative signed accumulation, or the modelled flow itself may be negative.
7. Signed area subtracts below-axis regions. Geometric area counts all regions positively.
8. The triangle has base 3 and height 6, so its area is \(9\).
9. \(x^3-4x+C\).
10. \([x^3-4x]_1^3=(27-12)-(1-4)=18\).
11. \(x^2+C\). Every vertically shifted function has derivative \(2x\), so the derivative cannot identify the original constant.
12. \(A'(x)=x^2+1\).
13. With \(x=[0,1,2]\), the estimate is \(0.5(0+1)+0.5(1+4)=3\). The exact value is \(8/3\), so the estimate is high by \(1/3\).
14. Each contribution is rate times its interval width. Treating unequal intervals as equal changes the modelled accumulation.
15. A long unobserved period may contain a spike or reversal that endpoint interpolation cannot capture.
16. Net change is \(\int_0^6(12-3t)dt=[12t-1.5t^2]_0^6=18\) cases. The rate crosses zero at 4 hours. Total absolute flow is \(\int_0^4(12-3t)dt-\int_4^6(12-3t)dt=24+6=30\) cases.
