# Chapter 41 Solutions

1. Unusual values can be legitimate, erroneous, novel, or harmful; investigation supplies the meaning.
2. A point is unusual alone, a contextual case is unusual only in its setting, and a collective anomaly is unusual as a related group or sequence.
3. An extreme batch pH is a point; that pH only for one recipe is contextual; a repeated oscillating sensor sequence is collective.
4. Unit, scoring time, available fields, reference, exclusions, capacity, confirmed outcome, and action ownership are valid elements.
5. Prefer a rule when the condition, authority, and required action are already known and stable.
6. Extremes distort the mean and standard deviation more strongly than the median and MAD.
7. Fill weight and viscosity can each be in range while their combination violates the normal process relationship.
