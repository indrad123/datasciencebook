# Chapter 10 Exercises

1. A filling line runs at 40 litres per minute for 6 minutes. Find the accumulated volume.
2. State the units of \(\int_0^5 r(t)\,dt\) when \(r\) is cases per hour and \(t\) is hours.
3. Approximate the area under values \(y=[2,4,6]\) at \(x=[0,1,2]\) with left rectangles.
4. Repeat Exercise 3 with right rectangles.
5. Repeat Exercise 3 with the trapezoidal rule.
6. Explain why a definite integral can be negative.
7. Distinguish signed area from geometric area.
8. Evaluate \(\int_0^3 2x\,dx\) geometrically.
9. Find an antiderivative of \(3x^2-4\).
10. Evaluate \(\int_1^3 (3x^2-4)\,dx\).
11. State the constant of integration in \(\int 2x\,dx\) and explain why it appears.
12. If \(A(x)=\int_0^x (t^2+1)\,dt\), find \(A'(x)\).
13. Estimate \(\int_0^2 x^2\,dx\) with two equal trapezoids and compare it with the exact result.
14. Explain why unequal time intervals must use their actual widths.
15. Describe one situation in which missing observations make numerical integration unreliable.
16. Challenge: Net inventory flow is \(r(t)=12-3t\) cases per hour for \(0\le t\le6\). Find the net inventory change and the total absolute flow.
