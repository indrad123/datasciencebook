# Chapter 8 Exercises

1. State the shape and entry \(a_{2,3}\) of \(A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\end{bmatrix}\).
2. Add \(\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{bmatrix}\) and \(\begin{bmatrix}5&6\\7&8\end{bmatrix}\).
3. Calculate the transpose of \(\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\end{bmatrix}\).
4. Multiply \(\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}42\\16\end{bmatrix}\).
5. State the output shape of a \(3\times4\) matrix multiplied by a \(4\times2\) matrix.
6. Explain why a \(2\times3\) matrix cannot multiply a two-component column vector.
7. Write \(2x+y=100\), \(x+3y=90\) in matrix form.
8. Solve that system by elimination.
9. Verify the solution using a residual vector.
10. Classify \(x+y=3\), \(2x+2y=8\).
11. Classify \(x+y=3\), \(2x+2y=6\).
12. Calculate the determinant of \(\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}\).
13. Explain why \(AB\) need not equal \(BA\).
14. Distinguish NumPy `A * B` from `A @ B`.
15. Explain why `np.linalg.solve(A, b)` is preferred to explicitly calculating `inv(A) @ b`.
16. Challenge: Describe what a small residual and a large condition number jointly tell you.
