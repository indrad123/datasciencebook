# Chapter 2 Exercises

1. Simplify \(42/56\).
2. Calculate \(3/5+1/4\).
3. A blend uses coffee and chicory in a ratio of 7:3. How many kilograms of each are needed for 80 kg?
4. A packing line produces 2,730 cartons in 6.5 hours. Calculate cartons per hour.
5. Supplier A charges IDR 840,000 for 30 kg. Supplier B charges IDR 1,092,000 for 42 kg. Which has the lower unit price?
6. Convert \(7/16\) to a decimal and percentage.
7. Find 18% of 2,450.
8. Demand rises from 1,250 to 1,475 cases. Calculate absolute and percentage change.
9. A late-delivery rate falls from 8% to 5%. Report the percentage-point and relative percentage changes.
10. Plant A passes 380 of 400 batches. Plant B passes 855 of 900. Find the combined pass rate.
11. Explain why a 30% increase followed by a 30% decrease does not return a value to its starting point.
12. State what should be reported when an indicator moves from zero to 25 and explain why the usual percentage-change formula cannot be used.
13. Write a Python call using the chapter functions to compare IDR 675,000 for 25 kg with IDR 495,000 for 18 kg.
14. A dashboard says, “Quality improved by 4%.” List two questions needed before interpreting the statement.
15. Challenge: Group A has a 90% pass rate and Group B has a 60% pass rate. Construct group sizes for which the combined rate is above 85%, then construct sizes for which it is below 65%.
