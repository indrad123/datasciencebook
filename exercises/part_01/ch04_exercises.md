1. Evaluate \(f(x)=3x+5\) at \(x=7\).
2. For \(C(q)=250{,}000+12{,}000q\), interpret the input, output, slope, and intercept.

5. State an operational domain for the number of whole pallets when capacity is 80 pallets.
6. Explain why \(g(x)=10/x\) excludes zero.
7. Determine whether the pairs \((1,4),(2,5),(1,7)\) define a function of x.
8. Find the intersection of \(A(q)=300{,}000+4{,}000q\) and \(B(q)=180{,}000+6{,}000q\), with costs measured in IDR.
9. Which plan in Exercise 8 is cheaper at 20 units? At 100 units?
10. Give one example of a non-linear business relationship.
11. Explain when connecting discrete points with a line may mislead.
12. List four items to inspect before interpreting a graph.
13. Explain why a truncated axis may change visual impression without changing the data.
14. Write Python code to generate quantities 0 through 100 in steps of 10 and calculate \(C(q)=250+12q\).
15. Challenge: A model is tested only for 0 to 200 cases. Explain the risk of using it at 5,000 cases.
