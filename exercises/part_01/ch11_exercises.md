# Chapter 11 Exercises

1. State the decision variable, objective, and constraint in choosing production quantity to minimise cost without exceeding capacity.
2. For \(L(w)=(w-4)^2+2\), calculate \(L(0),L(2),L(4),L(6)\).
3. Use the results of Exercise 2 as a grid search and select the best candidate.
4. Differentiate \(L(w)=(w-4)^2+2\).
5. Starting at \(w=0\), perform one gradient-descent step with learning rate 0.1.
6. Repeat Exercise 5 with learning rate 0.5. What happens?
7. Explain why a positive gradient suggests moving in the negative direction when minimising.
8. Distinguish a parameter from a hyperparameter.
9. Explain why zero gradient is not sufficient to prove a global minimum.
10. For \(f(x,y)=(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2\), find the gradient.
11. Perform one gradient step from \((0,0)\) with learning rate 0.25.
12. State two sensible stopping criteria.
13. Explain the difference between full-batch and stochastic gradients.
14. Describe what can happen when the learning rate is too large or too small.
15. Explain why training loss alone is not enough to choose a predictive model.
16. Challenge: Show that gradient descent on \((w-4)^2+2\) with learning rate 0.5 reaches the minimum in one update from any start.
