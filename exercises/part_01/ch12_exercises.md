# Chapter 12 Exercises

1. Define the sample space for one fair six-sided die.
2. Find the probability of rolling an even number.
3. If \(P(A)=0.35\), find \(P(A^c)\).
4. If disjoint events have probabilities 0.2 and 0.3, find their union probability.
5. If \(P(A)=0.5\), \(P(B)=0.4\), and \(P(A\cap B)=0.1\), find \(P(A\cup B)\).
6. Find \(P(A\mid B)\) from the values in Exercise 5.
7. Determine whether the events in Exercise 5 are independent.
8. Explain why mutually exclusive non-zero events are not independent.
9. An NRG alert has 90% sensitivity, 5% false-positive rate, and 2% defect prevalence. Find the posterior defect probability after an alert.
10. Express Exercise 9 using natural frequencies per 1,000 items.
11. Explain the base-rate error in saying that a 90% sensitive alert gives a 90% chance of defect.
12. A shipment loss is 0 with probability 0.8 and 100 with probability 0.2. Find expected loss.
13. Explain why expected loss need not be a possible individual outcome.
14. Distinguish aleatory uncertainty from uncertainty caused by limited knowledge.
15. Describe why simulation results should include a seed and number of trials.
16. Challenge: If alert sensitivity rises to 95% but false-positive rate rises to 10%, calculate the new posterior and compare it with Exercise 9.
