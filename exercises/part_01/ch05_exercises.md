# Chapter 5 Exercises

1. Evaluate \(2^6\).
2. Simplify \(x^4x^3\).
3. Simplify \(a^7/a^2\), stating the restriction.
4. Evaluate \(10^{-4}\).
5. Explain the difference between \((-4)^2\) and \(-4^2\).
6. Evaluate \(81^{1/2}\) and \(27^{1/3}\).
7. Calculate 12,000 growing by 6% for four periods.
8. Calculate 5,000 retaining 85% each period for three periods.
9. Rewrite \(2^8=256\) in logarithmic form.
10. Evaluate \(\log_{10}(100000)\).
11. Expand \(\log_b(xy^3)\).
12. Explain why \(\log(-5)\) is invalid in ordinary real arithmetic.
13. Find the time for 10,000 to double at 8% per period.
14. Distinguish adding 800 annually from growing 8% annually.
15. Write Python calls for compound_value(10000, 0.08, 5) and time_to_target(10000, 20000, 0.08).
16. Challenge: Explain why the continuous crossing time and the first whole reporting period may differ.
