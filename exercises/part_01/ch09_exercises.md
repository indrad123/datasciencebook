# Chapter 9 Exercises

1. NRG orders rise from 240 to 300 cases between 09:00 and 12:00. Calculate the average rate of change and state its units.
2. Explain why a total change and a rate of change answer different questions.
3. Find the slope between ((2,7)) and ((6,19)).
4. For (f(x)=x^2), calculate the difference quotient at (x=3) with (h=1).
5. Repeat Exercise 4 with (h=0.1). What value is the quotient approaching?
6. Use the limit definition to find the derivative of (f(x)=x^2).
7. Differentiate (5x^3-4x+7).
8. Differentiate ((2x+1)(x^2-3)) using the product rule.
9. Differentiate ((x^2+1)/(x+1)) using the quotient rule.
10. Differentiate ((3x-2)^4) using the chain rule.
11. If (C(q)=0.02q^2+15q+600), find and interpret (C'(100)).
12. Find the tangent line to (f(x)=x^2) at (x=3).
13. State where (f(x)=|x|) is not differentiable and explain geometrically.
14. Compare forward and central finite differences in plain language.
15. Explain why an extremely small numerical step can sometimes reduce accuracy.
16. Challenge: A forecast loss is (L(w)=(w-4)^2+2). Find (L'(w)), identify its stationary point, and explain why it is a minimum.
