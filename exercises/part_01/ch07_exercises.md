# Chapter 7 Exercises

1. State the dimension and components of \(\mathbf{x}=(3,-1,4)\).
2. Calculate \((4,3,5)+(2,4,6)\).
3. Calculate \((4,3,5)-(2,4,6)\) and interpret each sign.
4. Calculate \(-2(3,-1)\).
5. Find \(\lVert(3,4)\rVert_2\).
6. Normalise \((3,4)\) to a unit vector.
7. Calculate the dot product of \((1,2,3)\) and \((4,0,-1)\).
8. Are \((1,2)\) and \((-2,1)\) perpendicular? Justify using a dot product.
9. Calculate Euclidean distance between \((4,3,5)\) and \((2,4,6)\).
10. Calculate Manhattan distance for the same pair.
11. Calculate cosine similarity between \((1,2)\) and \((2,4)\).
12. Explain why cosine similarity is undefined for \((0,0)\) and \((1,2)\).
13. Explain how a rupiah-valued feature can dominate an unscaled distance calculation.
14. Explain why city codes 1, 2, and 3 should not automatically be compared with Euclidean distance.
15. Write Python calls to calculate Euclidean distance and cosine similarity for \((4,3,5)\) and \((2,4,6)\).
16. Challenge: Give two vectors that have cosine similarity 1 but a large Euclidean distance, and explain why.
