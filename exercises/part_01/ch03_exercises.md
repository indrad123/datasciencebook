# Chapter 3 Exercises

1. In \(C=12q+400\), identify the variables, coefficient, and constant.
2. Evaluate \(5x+7\) when \(x=9\).
3. Simplify \(8q+3-2q+11\).
4. Expand \(7(x+4)\).
5. Translate: “IDR 250,000 fixed cost plus IDR 18,000 for each carton.”
6. Solve \(x+13=42\).
7. Solve \(7x=161\).
8. Solve \(5x+30=280\).
9. Solve \(4x+90=2x+210\).
10. Classify \(3(x+2)=3x+6\) as one, no, or infinitely many solutions.
11. Classify \(2(x+5)=2x+13\).
12. Rearrange \(R=pq\) for \(p\).
13. Rearrange \(C=F+vq\) for \(F\).
14. NRG pays IDR 3,600,000 setup cost and IDR 24,000 per case. Its budget is IDR 27,600,000. Define the variable, form an equation, solve, and verify.
15. Freight plans cost \(A=500{,}000+3{,}000q\) and \(B=260{,}000+5{,}000q\) IDR. Find the equal-cost quantity and cost.
16. Explain why \(q=-12\) may be algebraically valid but invalid for production quantity.
17. Write a Python call to solve \(18x+120=1200\) using the chapter function.
18. Challenge: An equation gives \(p=42.3\) pallets. Explain what information is needed before deciding the dispatch quantity.
