Complete the exercises without reading the solutions manual. Show units at every calculation step.

1. Classify `12`, `kg`, `12 kg`, and mass.
2. Convert 3.5 kg to grams.
3. Convert 750 mL to litres.
4. Calculate the number of packs on a pallet with 40 cartons and 18 packs per carton.
5. Calculate the labelled mass of the pallet in Exercise 4 when each pack is 70 g. Report grams and kilograms.
6. Explain why `4 kg + 12 minutes` is incompatible.
7. Calculate average speed for 270 km travelled in 4.5 h.
8. Explain why a long software decimal does not prove measurement precision.
9. Calculate the total and mean of 499.8, 500.1, 500.0, 499.9, and 500.2 mL.
10. Implement Exercises 4 and 5 in Python with descriptive names.
