# Chapter 50 Exercises

1. Name the five conceptual parts of an artificial neuron.
2. Compute $z$ for inputs `(2, 3)`, weights `(0.5, -1)`, and bias `1`.
3. Explain the role of the bias.
4. Calculate the sigmoid of zero.
5. Contrast identity, step, and sigmoid activations.
6. Distinguish a predicted probability from an action threshold.
7. Interpret a positive moisture weight in the NRG example.
8. Explain why that weight is not a causal effect.
9. Calculate binary cross-entropy for `y=1` and `p=0.8`.
10. Describe one reason to clip probabilities in a loss calculation.
11. Explain what the learning rate controls.
12. Distinguish an epoch from a parameter update.
13. Write the vectorized preactivation for a batch.
14. Explain why zero initialization works for one neuron but causes a problem in multilayer networks.
15. Show why a single linear boundary cannot represent XOR.
16. Design an evaluation checklist for NRG's package-risk neuron.
