# Chapter 23 Exercises

1. Distinguish association, correlation, and regression.  
2. Why should a scatterplot usually precede a correlation coefficient?  
3. Calculate the sample covariance of $x=[1,2,3]$ and $y=[2,4,6]$.  
4. State the range and meaning of Pearson correlation.  
5. Explain why a correlation near zero can coexist with a strong relationship.  
6. When may Spearman correlation be preferable to Pearson correlation?  
7. Why does converting kilometres to metres not change Pearson correlation?  
8. Give an NRG example of correlation without agreement.  
9. Explain why correlation alone cannot establish causation.  
10. Fit a line through distances $[5,10,15,20]$ and times $[36,44,52,60]$.  
11. Interpret the fitted slope and intercept from Exercise 10.  
12. Define a residual and calculate the residual at 15 kilometres.  
13. What does $R^2$ describe, and what does it not establish?  
14. Explain leverage and why deleting a high-leverage point automatically is unsafe.  
15. Why is prediction at 100 kilometres risky when training distances end at 40 kilometres?  
16. Design a short NRG analysis plan relating delivery distance to time while separating association, explanation, and prediction.
