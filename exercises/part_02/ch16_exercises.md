# Chapter 16 Exercises: Centres, Quantiles, and Spread

Use the delivery times `2, 2, 3, 3, 5` days for Questions 1 to 6.

1. Calculate the arithmetic mean.
2. Calculate the median.
3. Identify the mode or modes.
4. Calculate the range.
5. Using linear interpolation at index `(n - 1)p`, find Q1, Q3, and the IQR.
6. Calculate the sample variance and sample standard deviation.
7. Add a 30-day delivery to the data. Recalculate the mean and median. Which changed more?
8. Explain why the median and IQR are often paired for a skewed distribution.
9. Region A has mean sales of 120 units across 40 stores. Region B has mean sales of 150 units across 10 stores. Find the overall mean.
10. Explain why averaging the two regional means without weights is wrong in Question 9.
11. For sorted values `10, 20, 30, 40`, calculate the 25th percentile with linear interpolation at `(n - 1)p`.
12. Why can two software packages report different sample quartiles for the same short dataset?
13. A delivery-time standard deviation is 1.8 days. Interpret its units and meaning without claiming that every observation lies within 1.8 days of the mean.
14. Two stores have the same mean daily sales but different standard deviations. What operational difference might this reveal?
15. State one limitation of the range and one limitation of the standard deviation.
16. Choose suitable summaries for a strongly right-skewed customer-spend distribution and justify the choice.
