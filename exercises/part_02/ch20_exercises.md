# Chapter 20 Exercises

1. Define a point estimate and an interval estimate.
2. Interpret a 95% confidence procedure using repeated sampling.
3. Explain why “95% probability that the fixed mean lies in this observed interval” is not the conventional frequentist interpretation.
4. Calculate a 95% normal interval for an estimate of 72 with standard error 4.
5. Find the margin of error in Exercise 4.
6. State what happens to interval width when confidence increases from 95% to 99%, with the data unchanged.
7. If sample size increases from 100 to 400, by what approximate factor does the standard error change?
8. Explain why a larger sample cannot correct a biased measurement instrument.
9. Calculate the sample proportion when 18 of 200 shipments are late.
10. Use the chapter result to report the 95% Wilson interval for Exercise 9.
11. Why can a Wald interval be troublesome near zero or one?
12. List the four main steps of a percentile bootstrap interval.
13. Why should bootstrap analysis record its random seed?
14. Explain why resampling individual observations may be unsuitable for clustered data.
15. A 95% interval for a late rate is 5.8% to 13.8%, and action is required above 12%. What does the interval imply for the decision?
16. Design a simulation that checks the coverage of a mean interval.
