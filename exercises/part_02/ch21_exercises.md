# Chapter 21 Exercises

1. Define a null hypothesis.
2. Define a p-value without using “the probability the null is true.”
3. Calculate $z$ for sample mean 104, null mean 100, and standard error 2.
4. Report the two-sided p-value for Exercise 3 from the chapter.
5. Explain why p=0.03 does not prove a checkout design is valuable.
6. Distinguish Type I and Type II errors.
7. Define statistical power.
8. Why are p=0.049 and p=0.051 not fundamentally different evidence?
9. Explain why failing to reject is not accepting the null.
10. A conversion rate rises from 8.0% to 8.5%. Calculate absolute and relative changes.
11. Calculate standardized difference when means are 12 and 10 and pooled SD is 4.
12. Why should a practical threshold be specified before analysis?
13. Outline a two-group permutation test.
14. Why must permutation preserve blocked assignment?
15. Calculate the Bonferroni per-test alpha for five tests and family alpha 0.05.
16. Draft a reporting checklist for an NRG experiment.
