# Chapter 22 Exercises

1. Define effect size.  
2. Calculate the mean difference between 95 and 100 minutes.  
3. Explain why natural units are usually reported first.  
4. Late rates change from 8% to 6%. Calculate risk difference and risk ratio.  
5. Calculate Cohen's $d$ for means 105 and 100 with pooled SD 10.  
6. Why are generic small-medium-large labels insufficient?  
7. Define a minimum practically important effect.  
8. Give one worthwhile threshold for an NRG delivery project.  
9. Define power.  
10. List four inputs that affect power.  
11. State the approximate power for $d=0.5$, alpha 0.05, and 64 per group.  
12. State the approximate per-group size for 80% power at $d=0.5$.  
13. Why can store clustering reduce effective sample size?  
14. Why is observed post-study power unhelpful?  
15. Distinguish statistical power from business value.  
16. Draft a sensitivity analysis for a proposed intervention.
