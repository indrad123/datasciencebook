# Chapter 14 Exercises

1. Classify product category as nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio.
2. Classify a five-level agreement response from strongly disagree to strongly agree.
3. Classify temperature measured in degrees Celsius.
4. Classify shipment weight in kilograms.
5. Explain why a numeric storage type does not determine a variable's measurement scale.
6. Distinguish primary data from secondary data using an NRG example.
7. State one advantage and one risk of administrative transaction data.
8. Explain simple random sampling in plain language.
9. For a frame of 1,000 orders, describe a systematic sample of 100 orders.
10. Explain when stratified sampling may be preferable to a simple random sample.
11. Explain why cluster sampling can reduce field cost but increase similarity within selected groups.
12. Distinguish sampling error from coverage error.
13. Distinguish random measurement error from systematic measurement bias.
14. A scale reports 10.3, 20.4, and 30.2 kg for reference weights of 10, 20, and 30 kg. Calculate the signed errors and average bias.
15. Give one example each of nonresponse error and processing error.
16. Challenge: NRG estimates customer satisfaction from a voluntary app survey that excludes offline customers and uses a leading question. Identify at least three error sources and propose one mitigation for each.
