# Chapter 24 Exercises

1. Classify warehouse, late status, picking time, and five-point rating by data type.  
2. What does a contingency table show?  
3. Calculate the expected count for a cell whose row total is 40, column total is 30, and grand total is 100.  
4. What does a chi-square test of independence assess?  
5. Why should conditional percentages accompany a chi-square statistic?  
6. Explain why sparse expected counts can be a problem.  
7. State the omnibus null hypothesis for one-way ANOVA.  
8. Distinguish between-group and within-group variation.  
9. Calculate F when MS_B is 24 and MS_W is 6.  
10. Calculate eta squared when SS_B is 60 and SS_T is 240.  
11. Why does a significant omnibus result not identify which groups differ?  
12. Why can independence not be verified from a residual plot?  
13. When may Welch ANOVA be preferable to classical one-way ANOVA?  
14. What does Kruskal-Wallis compare, and why is “a test of medians” incomplete?  
15. Explain exchangeability in a permutation test.  
16. Design an NRG plan to compare picking time across three shifts, including descriptive analysis, inference, effect size, and limitations.
